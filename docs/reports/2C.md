# Block 2C — presentation pass

Commit `4218849`. Verified against
https://hardscape-configurator.hardscape-configurator.workers.dev
Screenshots: `2c-layout.png`, `2c-scene.png` (compare with `2b-layout.png`).

Nothing new is modelled. `deriveWall`, `computeTakeoff` and the catalog are
untouched. Every change is to how what already exists is presented.

---

## 1. Camera

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Target | box inflated to 6.4 ft so the scale figure stayed framed | the wall's own bounding box |
| Fit | bounding sphere of that inflated box | distance that puts the wall at 0.68 of canvas width |
| Elevation | 15.6° (nominally right, but aimed at the wrong box) | 17° |
| Azimuth | 28.4° | 30° |
| Polar limits | 0.2 rad to 88° — a plan view was reachable | 55° to 88° |

The old rig was not badly angled so much as aimed at the wrong thing: forcing a
6.4 ft minimum height into the framing box, to keep the silhouette in shot, both
pushed the camera back and dragged the target upward, so the visitor got the top
of the retained fill. Framing the wall's own box and moving the figure into it
fixes both at once.

Width is fitted directly rather than via a bounding sphere. Turned 30° off the
face the run foreshortens and the end wall starts to show, so the silhouette to
fit is `runFt·cos(az) + depthFt·sin(az)`.

Projected width, computed through the same `PerspectiveCamera` the app builds,
at 800×900 canvas:

| Config | Distance | Width fraction |
|---|---|---|
| Garden wall, 20' × 3 | 43.6 ft | **0.691** |
| Backyard terrace, 40' × 6 | 85.5 ft | **0.692** |
| Driveway edge, 60' × 2 | 126.7 ft | **0.689** |
| Default, 24' × 4 | 52.0 ft | **0.692** |

`<Bounds>` is still not used, per SPEC §13.

## 2. Terrain and light

The straight cut where the ground stopped was the plane's outer edge at 160 ft,
sitting about 6° below the horizon: squarely mid-frame. The ground is now 4000 ft
across, which puts that edge within about 1° of the true horizon, where it reads
as a horizon.

**Fog was tried for this and reverted.** Depth fog does hide the edge, but a low
camera sees a great deal of distant ground, so all of it faded and the whole
image went pale and low-contrast — the opposite of "the wall face must be the
lightest, highest-contrast object in frame". A bigger plane solves the same
problem with no cost to the image.

Turf dropped in both value and chroma (`#8d9a80` → `#79806e`), and the terrace
top is now a shade *under* the lawn rather than over it, since the top of the
fill is the one part of the job nobody wants to look at.

The key light moved from roughly 42° elevation to about 17°, and further off to
the side. The 0.25" joint reveal has been in the geometry since 1B; nothing was
lighting it. Raking the key across the face is what makes the running bond and
the setback read without moving the camera in.

## 3. Scale figure

From the right-hand end at `[runFt + 2.4, 0, 5.5]` to `[runFt * 0.25, 0, 5]`:
in front of the wall, on the low ground, a quarter of the way along. At the end
of the run it was ambiguous whether she stood in front of the wall or on the
terrace behind it.

## 4. Left column

1344 px of content in a 900 px viewport, so everything from the presets down was
below the fold, including `Copy link`.

| Change | Saved |
|---|---|
| Product family: 6 stacked rows → wrapped chips | ~115 px |
| Locked styles: full cards → one-line chips | ~40 px |
| Active style cards: shorter swatch and padding | ~25 px |
| Colours: 2 columns → 3, smaller swatches | ~45 px |
| Header subtitle removed, section gaps tightened | ~40 px |
| `Finished height` + `Copy link` pinned to the column foot | 29 px, and never scrolls away |

Result: **900 / 900**. All ten required controls visible without scrolling, on
the public URL.

Nothing left the registry. All six families are still present with lock and
hours; they are chips instead of rows.

`Copy link` is pinned rather than merely fitted. SPEC §12 calls it the bridge to
lead capture, so its reachability should not depend on scroll position — now, or
after anything else is added to the column.

## Note on verification

The pixel-based "wall width" probe in the throwaway test script reports 0.999
and is wrong: it cannot separate wall from bank and soil by colour. The
authoritative figures are the projection table above, computed through the same
camera the app constructs. The probe was kept only as a smoke test for
`readPixels` succeeding.
